BONUS (optional, not graded) - hottest station on AVERAGE across its log

One approach with awk (average temp_f per file, then sort):
$ awk -F, 'FNR>1{s[FILENAME]+=$2;n[FILENAME]++} \
    END{for(f in s) print s[f]/n[f], f}' station_logs/*.csv | sort -n | tail -1
76.5833 station_logs/WHI03_2026-07-11.csv

An equivalent approach with a for loop:
$ for f in station_logs/*.csv; do
      avg=$(tail -n +2 "$f" | cut -d, -f2 | awk '{s+=$1} END{print s/NR}')
      echo "$avg $f"
  done | sort -n | tail -1

Answer: WHI03 (Reynolds, White County), ~76.6 F average - the hottest
station on average. Any correct method that aggregates across all the log
files and sorts is fine.
